# The future base, slice 1: arrival, a corridor with two rooms, one combat hall

> Written by the architect (Claude Fable 5.2) on 2026-11-13, after the owner rejected the first maps.
> Scope set by the owner: "the room where the player arrives after entering the portal, a corridor with a couple of rooms, and a hall to fight in. Only that."
> Nothing of the deleted plan is reused. This plan describes places first; pieces come second and only where a place asks for them.

## 1. What the owner asked for (his words, translated)
- A military base that is "mostly corridors, and those corridors lead to small rooms and then to large halls".
- Coherence: "floor textures go on the floor, with a logic". "It cannot all be flat: reliefs, ways up and down, floors that are grating, floors that are only metal. It will vary a lot by zone."
- "A texture that is one square repeated 80 times looks horrible."
- "The zones where fights happen need stairs and level changes."
- "The structure of the kits' own demo maps is your reference. Look at the textures, their shapes and drawings."
- "What matters is the level design" (on his reference images).

## 2. What the references teach
The owner's images (two Cyberpunk 2077 interiors, Halo Infinite, Halo CE Anniversary, and a full walk through a Halo Reach ship interior) and a search of developer talks and level-design texts on both series.

**Read from the owner's images**
- **A stair is a combat space.** Wide steps between solid parapets, light fixtures set into the parapets, enemies above.
- **Platforms at several heights**, joined by short stairs and ramps, with cover that belongs there (containers on a dock, crates by a lift).
- **Big structural ribs set the rhythm** of corridors and halls; they also hide the joints between modules.
- **Floors are drawn:** a plain central lane, side strips of another material, a trim in a second colour along every edge, anti-slip texture where people walk on a slope.
- **A ramp that rises to a door**, with a line of small lights on both edges.
- **The corridor bends** before the hall, so the hall is revealed, not seen from far away.
- **A hall has a centre:** a raised round platform with one hero object, a ring around it, low cover along the ring, a balcony above. It is seen through an opening from the corridor before the player walks in.
- **Dark, with pools of light** and one accent colour per zone.

**From the texts** (sources in the day entry)
- Halo's own numbers: the player is about 2.1 m tall and runs 6.9 m/s; the smallest path is 1.2 m wide.
- Main doors and corridors about twice the size of side ones; a 1 m difference does not read.
- Give the player a foothold to look over the arena before the fight, or the fight happens in the doorway.
- At most three floor heights per area. Going down is cheap; going up needs room for stairs and ramps.
- Low cover is 1.0 to 1.25 m, high cover 1.75 m or more; use less than you think.
- A full-height pillar in the middle makes the fight circle around it.
- Every light needs a visible fixture. Mark each room's function with architecture and one hero object before lighting it.
- Repeated props are noticed before repeated architecture.

## 3. The place
**Blackwall Station, sublevel 3: the Anomaly wing.** The base was built over the castle's seal, and this wing exists to keep the seal contained and watched. Everything in it follows from that: a chamber around the anomaly, a lock between it and the rest of the base, a room that watches it, a store for what the watch needs, and a transfer bay where sealed containers leave the wing.

The wing is working military architecture, not a showroom: dark painted metal and concrete, services in the open (cable trenches under grating, pipes and trays under the ceiling), hazard paint only at real hazards. **Accent colour of the wing: amber** (hazard, lit grates). The anomaly itself is the one cold, bright thing in it.

### 3.1 Containment chamber (where the player arrives)
- **What it is:** a pit built around the seal. The seal lies at the bottom, on a round plate. Four emitter pylons stand around it and cables run from them, under the grating, to the walls.
- **Shape:** a tall square room, about 14 x 14 m and 8 m high. The pit is 9 m across and 1.2 m below the walkway that rings it.
- **Level changes:** the player wakes in the pit (the low point of the whole wing) and climbs out by one of two short stairs to the ring walkway. From the ring, a ramp rises 0.6 m to the lock door. The first thing the player does in the future is climb.
- **Floors:** pit floor, solid plate with a round trim around the seal and hazard paint on the pit edge. Ring walkway, grating (the cable trenches are under it), with a solid trim along the pit and the walls. Ramp, anti-slip plate with a light line on both edges.
- **Walls and ceiling:** heavy ribs in the corners and mid-wall. One wall has the long window of the monitoring room, 2 m above the ring: the player sees that someone was watching. The ceiling is dark, with a ring of fixtures over the pit.
- **Hero object:** the seal and its four pylons. **Light:** cold white-blue from the seal and the pit ring; amber at the door.
- **No fight here.** It is the safe room and the portal.

### 3.2 The lock
- **What it is:** the decontamination lock between the anomaly and the base. Small and low on purpose (about 5 x 6 m, 3.2 m high): the squeeze before the corridor.
- **Floor:** rubber anti-slip with a drain grate down the middle. **Walls:** nozzles and a warning strip. Two heavy doors, one at each end.

### 3.3 The spine corridor
- **What it is:** the wing's main corridor. About 5 m wide and 4.5 m high, in an L: 14 m, a right-angle bend, then 18 m to the hall. The bend hides the hall.
- **Rhythm:** a rib every 4 m. Between two ribs the wall is either a plain panel, a service panel (pipes, a vent) or a door.
- **Floor:** a solid plate lane 3 m wide down the middle; a 1 m strip of grating on each side over the cable trench; a trim between lane and grating. At the bend, one full bay of grating over a junction pit.
- **Ceiling:** an open tray of pipes and cables on one side, light fixtures at every rib on the other.
- **Light:** dim, with a pool at each rib; brighter at the far end, where the hall is.
- **Two side doors**, both half the size of the main doors.

### 3.4 Monitoring room (first side room)
- **What it is:** the watch post over the anomaly. Its long window looks down into the containment chamber the player just left.
- **Shape:** about 6 x 9 m, 3.5 m high, floor 0.6 m above the corridor (three steps up at its door) so the window clears the chamber's ring.
- **Floor:** raised access floor, clean panels. **Hero object:** the console desk under the window, screens lit. Chairs pushed back, one fallen: the watch left in a hurry.
- **Pickup:** provisional, one health item on the desk.

### 3.5 Store room (second side room)
- **What it is:** the wing's store: racks, sealed crates, a caged locker for weapons.
- **Shape:** about 6 x 7 m, 3.5 m high, floor 0.4 m below the corridor (a short ramp down, for trolleys).
- **Floor:** bare concrete with a painted lane from the door to the cage. **Light:** one strip, half the room in shadow.
- **Pickup:** provisional, ammunition in the cage.

### 3.6 Transfer bay (the combat hall)
- **What it is:** where sealed containers from the anomaly are loaded onto the freight lift. That is why it has a lift pit, lanes painted on the floor, stacked containers, an overhead rail and a supervisors' catwalk.
- **Shape:** about 30 x 24 m, 10 m high.
- **Three floor heights:**
  - **+1.5 m, the entry landing.** The corridor arrives here, on a balcony 8 m wide. The player sees the whole bay before going down. Stairs go down on both sides, one straight and one with a landing and a turn.
  - **0 m, the bay floor.** Two lanes around the central pit, with low cover (crates, 1.1 m) along them and three tall blocks (containers, 2.6 m) that break the long sight lines.
  - **-1.0 m, the lift pit** in the centre, 10 x 8 m, with the lift platform as hero object. One ramp and one short stair lead into it. It is the low ground: dangerous to stay in, quick to cross.
  - **+3.5 m, the catwalk** along the far wall and one side, reached by one stair at the far end. It is the high ground and the reward for crossing the bay. (This is a fourth height, but it never mixes with the entry landing: from any spot the player deals with three.)
- **Floors:** landing and catwalk, grating; bay floor, concrete with painted lanes and hazard paint around the pit; pit, steel plate; stairs, anti-slip.
- **Loops:** around the pit on the bay floor (both ways), and up the far stair, along the catwalk and down by a drop onto a container. No dead ends.
- **Enemies** come in through two doors on the far wall and one on the catwalk, never behind the player.
- **Exits:** the big freight door on the far wall (closed in this slice: it is where the base continues).
- **Light:** brighter than the corridor. Amber around the pit, white work lights over the lanes, the catwalk in half shadow.

## 5. Rules for building it
- No surface is one texture repeated. A floor is made of zones (lane, strip, trim, threshold), each with the material its use asks for.
- Every prop answers "why is this here". No scattering.
- Every room is rendered from the player's eye and reviewed before the owner sees it.
- Collision is simple and matches what is seen.

## 6. What is built (version 1, 2026-11-13) and what is not
Map: `/Game/Glyphbound/Maps/FutureBase`, built by `Tools/Jam/Build-FutureBase.py`. Frame: +X is the way the player advances, the corridor's axis is Y = 0, every main floor is Z = 0.

**How it is built.** The kits' demo maps were read piece by piece (`Design/Levels/kits/htb_demo.json`), and whole *sections* of the High Tech Base demo are placed again exactly as their author assembled them: the control room, two corridor bays, the hangar shell, each with its own lights and the demo's exposure. On top of those shells, our level design is placed piece by piece from the Modular SciFi Station kit (a true 1 / 2 / 4 m grid kit: decks, truss posts, railings, stairs) and from High Tech Base props.

**Added after the owner's first walk (2026-11-13):**
- **Medium room** through the far door of the air plant's gallery, 4 m above the corridor (22 x 32 m): the lab kit's own room, placed as its author built it, **without the alien figures, their holograms and their tubes** (owner, 2026-11-14: "keep the room, but take out the figures and the holograms and put in assets that leave it a generic room"; those are the look of the Valkyrie's fight). In their place, more of what the room already has: cabinets, barrels and a work desk with its screens along the long wall, a second control panel and a cabinet on the dais. No fight here for now (owner). Between 2026-11-13 and 2026-11-14 a muster hall from the base's kit stood here instead: the architect had read "do not use those assets" as "not this kit", and its entry was walled up by one of its own backing boxes.
- **Both freight doors work** (`AGBDoor`: they lift while the player is near). Beyond the first, one more corridor bay; beyond the second, the **long service corridor**: the Spaceship Corridor Damaged model (ArmTm, Fab), the asset the owner meant by "there is already an asset like that". 5.3 m wide, 3.3 m high, 40.5 m long: a grid floor with loose cables on it, pipes and red-lit vents under the ceiling, a light strip down the middle, wall screens that show the containment seal's status (our own picture), dark windows, fire extinguishers and axes in wall niches, two crates, and the model's own double door at the far end, shut for now (the next slice starts there). It replaced a first version built from the station kit. The model's procedural wear does not exist in Unreal: its walls are plain painted metal, the look the owner approved from renders before the import.
- **Sub-boss hall** (owner, 2026-11-14) behind the long corridor's far door, whose two leaves now slide apart when the player comes near. 28 x 36 m, 12 m high, from the base's kit: two tiers of its 6 m wall modules, the freight branch's floor strips and lit ceiling, a freight door in the far wall that stays shut until the sub-boss is beaten (progress flag `FutureSubBossDefeated`, which nothing sets yet). **Galleries** 4 m up and 4 m wide along both side walls, joined over the entry by a bridge, with the catwalk's grated deck, posts and mesh railing: enemies walk and shoot from there. **No stair: the way up is the crates.** The player's jump rises 2 m; every step is 1.5 m or less: floor, one crate (1.5 m), two crates (3 m), gallery (4 m), through a gap in the railing. Each gallery has two such piles; a stepped pile of one, two and three crates stands in the far corner; thirteen more crates are cover on the floor, some stacked, with the middle left clear for the fight. No sub-boss yet.
- **The bay's stairs:** the west one starts 2.5 m inside the bay, clear of the corridor's last frame; the east one ends 2.5 m short of the end wall, with its foot lit and nothing in front of it.
- **Thresholds:** a steel plate in every doorway where one kit's floor meets another's. The builder checks its walks through every connection for holes, and since 2026-11-14 also for anything standing across them at chest height (doors that open are left out).
- **Pits have a way out on foot:** crate steps in the alcove's service pit (both sides of the bridge) and at both ends of the bay's trench.
- **The map starts in the future** (tag `GBStartFuture` on its player start).

**Materials.** High Tech Base pieces keep the materials their author gave each piece. The Modular SciFi Station is painted white in its pack (a Mars station); here every one of its materials except glass, lamps and signs uses a darker instance of itself (`MI_GB_*` in `/Game/Glyphbound/Materials/Kit`), so its steel reads as service steel and its rooms as painted grey-blue, not as a clean room.

**Collision.** Every kit piece blocks exactly as it looks (complex collision used as simple), except decals, cables and lamps.

**Review.** `gb.Smoke tour` (new) puts the player on each of the map's twenty-seven review spots, checks the floor and takes a screenshot through the real game camera.
