## What the game does today
Start in the past with the Apprentice staff, 3 spells in the loadout and double jump. The arena has: a movement course (double jump steps, a dash gap), a central portal (G to travel; past and future look different), a pistol and consumables near the start, four spell books (all 13 spells), a chest (ember staff, sword hilt, gems), two training dummies, a past wave encounter and a future one, an era-gated room (shotgun; closed in the past, open in the future) and a future-only ramp that only exists once the past wave is cleared (rifle and armor chest).
Controls are GDD 9. Menus: Tab inventory (pages Items / Weapons / Spells / Skills; Q/E or click a tab), K skill trees, Esc pause; the mouse works in every menu. Block (hold Q, or L2 on a gamepad) raises a summoned ice shield. Console: `gb.ReloadData`, `gb.QTE 0` / `gb.QTE 1`, `gb.Smoke`.
Since the first play-test: weapons are owned (a pickup goes to the hands only if its slot is empty; equip from the Weapons page), structures are 3D boxes with pixel tiles (`GBPlaceholder::SetupTexturedShape`), every projectile explosion has a shockwave with knockback, enemies path by a navmesh built at runtime (`AGBTestArena::BuildNavigation`) with local steering as fallback (`AI/GBSteering`), falling below `KillZ` (Game.json) kills, damage numbers come from the global event `UGBCombatLibrary::OnDamageDealt()`.

## Known limits (accepted for now)
- Enemies do not attack spell walls on purpose.
- Pixel tiles on a wall placed diagonally stretch up to about 1.4x (world-aligned projection picks one axis).
- Enemies never drop down a ledge higher than `EnemySteerLedgeDrop` (150 cm) while steering locally; on the navmesh they follow the mesh.
- A dropped gun comes back fully loaded when picked up again.
- Spell cooldowns persist through death.
- `gb.*` console commands do not exist in Shipping builds (we package Development).
- The warm Editor boot did not get faster with fewer plugins (about 10-11 s either way).
