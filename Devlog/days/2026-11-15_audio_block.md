# 2026-11-15: Audio for the whole game (audio session, Claude Sonnet 5.5)

Task from the orchestrator: the ASSETS machine delivered all the game's sounds; assign every needed sound and the music.

## What arrived
- `Incoming/ASSETS/20261116-002002_Bibliotecas_Sonidos`: 190 WAV (Medie bits 60, SciFiCoreSounds 130), all from **Soundsnap**
  (source confirmed by the team member; no per-file IDs, authors or license files; license terms to confirm by the owner).
- Four generated boss tracks (ElevenLabs Music on fal.ai, delivered 2026-11-13), 150 to 180 s each.
- The **main theme**, made by hand by a team member: composed for acoustic guitar, six guitar parts programmed in
  Guitar Pro 6 and exported, sound effects added in Audacity, plus a couple of effects recorded on a phone from the
  guitar. Guitars only. It plays on the main menu and at the start of the game (PastCastle).

## What was built (decisions)
- **Data-driven.** `Content/Data/Audio.json` maps a sound key to SoundWave assets and play settings (volume, pitch range,
  attenuation range, cooldown). It is generated by `Tools/Jam/audio_defs.js`, which also converts the sources to
  44.1 kHz 16-bit WAV under `Glyphbound/SourceArt/Audio/` (mono for 3D sounds, stereo for 2D). Retuning a sound or
  swapping a file is an edit to that script (or the JSON) and needs no recompile.
- `Tools/Jam/import_audio.py` recreates the SoundWave imports under `/Game/Glyphbound/Audio/<category>/` (Content is gitignored).
- **Code.** A small `GBAudio` namespace lives in `GBCombatLibrary.cpp` (no new header, so Live Coding can apply it):
  `Play(key)` picks a random file (never the same one twice in a row), a random pitch in the key's range and a slightly
  random volume, applies the key's cooldown against stacking, and plays 3D with an attenuation of the key's range, or 2D.
  `UpdateMusic` runs twice a second from the game mode: menu music, map music, a Valkyrie boss track while one is alive
  within 55 m, and an ambience loop per map, all crossfaded.
- Hooks (one line each, `Tools/Jam/audio_patch.js`): weapon attacks (`GBHeldItem`, keyed by weapon id), reload, spell
  casts (keyed by spell id) and failed casts, damage hits (player pain, sword, element, flesh/machine), explosions,
  enemy windup / alert / death, boss death, pickups, chests, doors, portals, breakables, torches (looping 3D), UI
  navigation and confirm, player death, footsteps (past or future surface by map).
- Why not SoundCues: a JSON table plus one function was faster to write and to retune than dozens of cue assets.
