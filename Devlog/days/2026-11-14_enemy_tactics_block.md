## Diagnosis first (the architect, with engine facts, before anyone wrote code)

### "They teleport through everything": the animation, not the movement
The symptom (3 m in half a second, back to where it was, again and again, when the player takes cover) is a mesh leaving its capsule. Measured in the Editor with `AnimationLibrary.get_bone_pose_for_time` on every clip of the robot and the orc: **the root bone never moves and the pelvis carries the clip's whole travel**.

| Clip | Length | Pelvis, last frame minus first |
|---|---|---|
| A_Robot_Rifle_Jog_Fwd | 1.53 s | +1061 cm forward |
| A_Robot_Rifle_Walk_Fwd | 1.53 s | +518 cm |
| A_Robot_Rifle_Walk_Bwd | 1.2 s | -400 cm |
| A_Robot_Rifle_Walk_Left / Right | 1.23 / 1.5 s | 411 / 500 cm sideways |
| A_Orc_Unarmed_Jog_Fwd | 1.77 s | +1237 cm |
| A_Orc_Attack_01 / ChargedAttack | 1.0 / 1.83 s | +175 / +166 cm |

The template mannequin's clips are root-motion clips; yesterday's retarget switched the retargeter's Root Motion op off (it laid the characters on their backs), so the travel stayed in the pelvis. When a robot lost sight of the player it started to "advance", the jog clip played, and its body ran 10 m ahead of its capsule and snapped back at the loop; sight flickering at a cover edge restarted it several times in two seconds. Yesterday's smoke could not see it: it checked that capsules moved and took still screenshots. The fix is in the animation code (subtract the clip's linear drift from the pelvis; the same measurement also gives each clip's true ground speed, which replaces the guessed `WalkAnimSpeed` / `RunAnimSpeed`), with a smoke check that fails on the old code: the pelvis must stay within 60 cm of the capsule while moving.

### Seen and shot through walls: one-sided collision
A second, separate fault found while looking for the first. A grid of 142,800 short traces across the map, each traced both ways: **1,700 segments on 51 kit meshes stopped a trace from one side only** (SM_Wall_01 alone 632). The kit's meshes are single-sided shells and the map uses their triangles as collision (complex as simple): from the back, a line of sight or a hitscan shot passes. Capsules were still stopped (other faces back them), so nobody walked through, but an enemy outside a room saw the player inside, was "aware", and fired. Fixed in the assets: `double_sided_geometry` on the 150 mesh assets the map blocks with (also in `Build-FutureBase.py`). The flag is cooked with the mesh, so it only shows after the Editor reloads the assets; the grid test is re-run then (result below).

Two things learned on the way: a level's collision is not ready in the same script run that loads it (the first grid run found nothing at all), and a theory is not a diagnosis (the architect's first guess for the teleport was this collision fault; the owner's second description and the clip measurements said otherwise).

## Design (GDD v0.57, section 10)
Techniques named in the brief (`Prompts/enemy_tactics_ai.md`) so they are used on purpose: tactical position selection by scoring sampled points (Killzone's position evaluation, UE's EQS; ours in C++ on the runtime navmesh, no assets), cover with a firing spot beside it, **attack tokens** (F.E.A.R., Halo, DOOM 2016: only two or three fire at once, the others move), squad roles (base of fire, two flanks, assault), a shared **last known position** with search, the melee circle of slots, a leash zone per encounter, and enemies that are prespawned and patrol instead of popping in.

## What was built

### Map and art (architect)
- **The glass bulkhead** (`Tools/Blender/bulkhead_glass_export.py`, `Tools/Jam/import_bulkhead.py`). Before: one scaled cube with a cloud texture and painted seams, which vanished when broken. Now a modelled piece in the manner of the deep wing's windows and the stairwell's portholes: a heavy steel frame that stays (jambs on plinths, lintel, cut corners, bolt heads; 836 polygons) and a glazing that breaks (eight panes of glass 10 cm thick with cut corner plates, in steel bars with bolted hub plates; 1,484 polygons). Material `M_GB_DeepGlass`: opaque like the windows it copies, nearly black teal with the deep water's glow behind, darker towards each pane's frame (thick glass passes less light there), grime along the edges and the foot of every pane (the panes are unwrapped 0..1 for this), streaks that ran down, and a gloss the grime kills. The first pass was a wall of bright cyan clouds (kept in media as the honest middle step); glow cut to a third, cloud contrast flattened, the per-pane falloff added. The steel is the worn corridor's own material.

The same pass fixed what yesterday's in-game render already showed: pieces are grouped into whole pieces before being sorted into slots (helmet jaws had gone to the chest, breastplate plates to a forearm), belts hang from the waist, and every plate is cut finer and pushed out to just outside the body (skin showed through chests, backs and thighs). 36 skeletal meshes, four sets x nine slots, imported with no errors. ### Code (Sonnet subagent from `Prompts/enemy_tactics_ai.md`; 26 files, about 5,000 lines added)
- **Clips in place** (`UGBEnemyAnimInstance`): at setup each clip's pelvis drift is measured; while playing, the drift for the clip's time is subtracted, so the body keeps its bob and stays over the capsule. The same number gives each clip's true ground speed (the guessed `WalkAnimSpeed` / `RunAnimSpeed` are only a fallback now) and the play rate is clamped (0.7 to 1.35: a little foot slide is better than slow motion). The orc's charged swing steps back before it lunges, so attack overlays are pinned to their first frame instead. The new smoke check (pelvis within 60 cm of the capsule while moving) reads 16 to 35 cm; the old code gave 500 to 1,000. It also caught the implementer's own slip on the way: the crouch, sprint and patrol clips at first had no drift removal (119 and 732 cm).
- **Movement**: three gaits (patrol, combat, run), acceleration and braking on path following, a turn rate, avoidance between ground enemies; the random strafe hop is gone. They run facing where they run and face the player when they walk in combat or fire.
- **Zones** (`AGBEncounter::GetZone`): trigger box and spawn points plus a margin; every move goal of walkers, melee enemies and flyers is clamped into it. Flyers needed a braking-aware stop at the edge and a ceiling (they overshot and floated above the zone).
- **Already there**: an encounter marked `Prespawn` spawns its first wave when the map loads, unaware, each spawn on `Patrol`, `Guard` or `Dormant`. The fight starts when one of them notices the player: sight cone held for `NoticeTime`, the player's gunfire heard (`HearingRange`; one call in the gun's fire function), damage, or a squadmate's alert, which goes round the squad with 0.2 to 1.0 s between members after a short beat in which the first one stops and turns. Later waves come in at spawn points the player cannot see, the furthest first, already alerted, with no "Wave N / M" message.
- **The squad** (`UGBSquadSubsystem`, one per encounter; pure rules in `GBTactics`, tested): the shared last known position, roles (base of fire, two flanks, assault, anchor), reserved positions, attack tokens (2 at once, 3 for five or more; flyers count half), melee slots on a circle (2 attackers at once), orbit slots for flyers. The position query samples about 36 points on the navmesh inside the zone, tests cover and a firing spot beside it with traces, scores them with weights from `Game.json`, measures the real path for the best three, and runs once per squad tick.
- **A ranged member**: holds in cover (crouched when it has the crouch clip), peeks to fire one or two bursts when it holds a token, and relocates when its cover is flanked for 1.2 s, the player is too close, it has stayed long enough, it is badly hurt, its role changes or it has had no line for 3 s. When the player hides, the base of fire shoots at where he was and the others close in.
- **The horde chief** (`AGBShieldEnemy`): scale 1.65, anchor of his squad. The shield works through the attribute component's incoming-damage hook, so bullets, projectiles and spells are all blocked inside a 130 degree frontal arc while it is raised, and a box as big as the shield stops the player's shot traces. He walks at the player behind it turning 110 degrees a second, lowers it for 1.3 s to aim, fires one shot of 30, stays open 0.8 s, and waits 3.5 s. 160 blocked damage in 3 s, a lightning stun or a freeze knock the shield aside for 2.5 s. Up close he bashes.
- **Console**: `gb.God [0|1]`, `gb.OpenValkyrie`, `gb.OpenDoors`. A test start: `-AllWeapons` on the command line gives every weapon and 12 magazines (the architect, at the owner's request for a test package).
- **Bugs the implementer found in its own work on the way** (each caught by a smoke check or a TACTICS log line): roles reassigned every tick made everyone relocate without end; a burst that began while a position answer was in flight froze in its telegraph and kept its token; an orbit slot behind a wall sent drones over the arena wall; patrol routes failed in rooms whose navmesh tile was not built yet (now retried, then local steering).
